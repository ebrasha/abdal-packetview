## 1. Introduction

Abdal PacketView is an Android application developed by Abdal Security Group for network traffic viewing, diagnostics, learning, and cybersecurity awareness.

This Privacy Policy explains how Abdal PacketView handles user data, network traffic information, permissions, and privacy related matters.

By using Abdal PacketView, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Data Collection

Abdal PacketView does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The app does not require user registration, does not ask for personal identity information, and does not transmit user data to our servers.

In the current version, Abdal PacketView does not use third party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## 3. Network Traffic and Packet Data

Abdal PacketView is designed to help users view and analyze network related information on their own device for legitimate purposes such as diagnostics, education, and cybersecurity awareness.

Network packets and traffic related data may include technical information such as IP addresses, ports, protocols, DNS queries, headers, and connection details.

This information is processed locally on the user's device. Abdal PacketView does not upload captured packet data or traffic information to any external server.

Users are responsible for using the app only on networks, devices, and traffic they own or are authorized to inspect.

## 4. Locally Stored Data

Abdal PacketView may store app settings, preferences, logs, or user generated capture data locally on the device.

Any locally stored data remains under the user's control. Users can delete locally stored data by using app options, clearing app data from Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

## 5. App Permissions

Abdal PacketView may request Android permissions required for network diagnostics, packet viewing, local storage, or app functionality.

Permissions are used only to provide the app's intended features. Abdal PacketView does not use permissions for unauthorized tracking, surveillance, or background data collection.

## 6. Third Party Services

The current version of Abdal PacketView does not use third party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking services.

If third party services are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Data Sharing

Abdal PacketView does not share personal data, packet data, logs, or user generated content with third parties.

We may only disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Abdal PacketView is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 9. Security

We care about user privacy and security. Abdal PacketView is designed to process data locally whenever possible.

However, no software, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should handle captured network data carefully, because packet data may contain sensitive technical information.

## 10. User Responsibility

Abdal PacketView must be used only for lawful, ethical, and authorized purposes.

Users must not use the app to monitor, capture, inspect, or analyze network traffic without proper permission.

Abdal Security Group is not responsible for misuse of the app by users.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published in the official GitHub repository or other official distribution channels.
